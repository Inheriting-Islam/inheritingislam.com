How the number works

What is published, and what is not.

A certificate is only worth something if it can be checked. It is only decent if checking it does not turn our students into a public list.

The number

What it looks like

Printed at the bottom right of every certificate, in the form IQ-Q-1448-014 — the house, the stage (Q, T or Ḥ), the Hijri year, and the number within that year.

The register

What it will show

The number, which stage it certifies, and the month it was issued. Enough to confirm a certificate in your hand is real.

Never

What it will not show

No student names. A number that is not listed was not issued by us; a number that is listed can be matched against the name on the paper you are holding. That is enough, and a public directory of who could not read is not.

A certificate is not an ijāzah

Every one says so on its face. These certify what a student demonstrated to a teacher — read unseen text, applied tajwīd through a juz, recited it from memory. None of them confers a licence to transmit, and none of them should ever be presented as one.

If a number does not check out

Write to hamza@inheritingislam.com with the number and we will tell you plainly. If somebody has put their own name on our work, we want to know before you do.
