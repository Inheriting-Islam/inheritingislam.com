About the house

Two people, one idea, and no intention of pretending otherwise.

Inheriting Islam is not an agency with a Muslim marketing angle. It is a Muslim household that builds — and the work is organised around a single sentence.

"The deen is a mīrāth. We received it, we live it, and our whole job is to pass it on well."

Everything in this house is one answer to the same question — how do you pass it on? A class in a masjid. A website that lets a community's work be seen. Software a student can trust with their time. A conversation recorded before the person who lived it is gone. Same inheritance, different hands.

The founder

Hamza Abdul-Tawwaab

Former imam · builder · Los Angeles roots · Morocco-based

I taught Qur'an and led taraweeh before I ever wrote a line of code. That is not a line for a website — it is the order the two things happened in, and it decides how I build.

I have taught at Islah LA in Los Angeles, Midtown Mosque in Memphis, Masjid As-Sabur in Las Vegas, and Atlanta Masjid in Atlanta. Four cities, four communities, and the same thing needed in each of them.

Standing in front of a congregation teaches you something no design course does: you can tell within thirty seconds whether people are with you or being talked at. Websites are the same. Most masjid websites are talking at people.

This house is kept deliberately small, because I would rather do a small amount of work properly than take on more than we can carry well. When capacity is full, I say so.

My roots are in Los Angeles and I am currently based in Morocco. The Studio's clients are American, the banking is American, and the calls happen on your clock, not mine.

How we work

The rules we are willing to be held to.

Written down so that you can hold us to them, and so that we can be reminded of them when a shortcut is tempting.

  1. One

    We do not claim a team we do not have

    It is two co-founders. There is no "team" page here because there is no team, and you would find out eventually.

  2. Two

    Nothing is described as finished until it is

    Not an app, not a client site, not a podcast season. Where the honest word is "in development" or "not live yet," that is the word we use, even on our own flagship project.

  3. Three

    Free to learn and to worship

    We do not paywall the fard. Where we charge, it is for convenience — or it is charged to those who can carry it, so that those who cannot are not asked to.

  4. Four

    We do not sell data. Ever.

    No surveillance advertising, no analytics resale, no list rental. This website itself carries no trackers, no cookies, and no third-party scripts. You can check the network tab.

  5. Five

    The premium work pays for the khidma work

    Businesses and large centres pay a full rate. Small masajid get the same care at a price they can carry, shown as a visible discount rather than quietly given. That is the arrangement, and it is the only reason both are possible.
