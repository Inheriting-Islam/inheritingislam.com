What we keep finding

The work is real. The internet keeps it invisible.

Masajid and Muslim nonprofits carry the weight of whole neighborhoods and then get judged by a website nobody has been able to edit since 2019. None of this is anyone's fault. All of it is fixable.

The proof

Sixteen pages for Al-Maun Neighborly Needs.

The humanitarian arm of Masjid As-Sabur — the oldest masjid in Las Vegas — serving the Historic Westside since 2003. One client, told properly, is worth more to you than a wall of logos.

  • Sixteen custom pages, written and built from their real material — programs, impact, events, giving, in memoriam, the lot.
  • Migrated off Squarespace, with a redirect mapped from every old address so no link, bookmark, or search result breaks.
  • Donations left where they already work. We did not move their money to prove a point.
  • Their own photographs — the AI-generated placeholders were thrown out and replaced with real Al-Maun community photos.
  • An automated test suite that checks every page for broken links, mobile overflow and load errors — run before handoff, and again after every change.

Where it stands today

Built, tested, and handed over: sixteen pages, real content, forms wired, redirects written. Go-live is the last mile on their side — the domain switch and two account keys. We do not call a site live until it is.

How it goes

Four steps, and you own everything at the end.

No retainer to talk to us. No proprietary platform you can never leave. If you ever want to take the site and go, you take the site and go.

  1. 01

    A twenty-minute call

    You tell us what the community actually needs. We tell you honestly whether we are the right people, and roughly what it would cost. No deck, no pitch.

  2. 02

    A fixed quote, in writing

    Scope, price, and a date window — two weeks, four, or six, whichever suits your calendar. Fifty percent to begin, the balance at launch. The price does not move because we worked faster than expected.

  3. 03

    We build and migrate

    Real content, real photographs, moved off whatever you are renting, tested page by page before anyone sees it. You review it on a private link while it is being built.

  4. 04

    Launch — and then we stay

    We train your team, hand over every login, and pick up the phone in month eight. That last part is the care plan, and it is the part that most agencies quietly stop doing.

Who is behind this

"I taught Qur'an and led taraweeh before I ever wrote a line of code."

That is the whole reason this house exists. I taught at Islah LA in Los Angeles, Midtown Mosque in Memphis, Masjid As-Sabur in Las Vegas, and Atlanta Masjid in Atlanta — teaching people to read the Book of Allah. Then I learned to build software, and found that the same job was waiting for me there.

The deen is a mīrāth. We received it, we live it, and our whole job is to pass it on well. Sometimes that is a class in a masjid. Sometimes it is a website that lets a masjid's work be seen and funded. Sometimes it is software a student can trust with their time. It is all the same inheritance, handed on with different hands.

My wife Khadija co-founded this house with me and runs client care and operations. It is the two of us, and we intend to keep it that way. So we are careful about what we say yes to — and we finish what we start.

Hamza Abdul-Tawwaab Founder · Inheriting Islam
