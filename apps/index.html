The Apps · lived

We were building seven. We stopped four.

Seven apps and two people was a list, not a plan. So four are no longer being built, and this page names them rather than quietly deleting the rows. Of the three that are left, one is in private beta and two are being built — and none of them have shipped. When one does, this page will say so, and not a day earlier.

See the three Ask to be told when one is ready

Built one at a time, well, by a two-person house. Seven half-finished apps were easier to announce and worth nothing to you — which is exactly why there are now three.

How the apps relate to the house

Each keeps its own name. We publish, we do not brand over it.

Jadhr is Jadhr. Sakīnah is Sakīnah. The house is the publisher standing behind them — the line at the bottom of the screen that reads "An Inheriting Islam app." The name you trust is the app's; the accountability is ours.

Why we build them at all

Every one of these started the same way: somebody in the house needed a tool, went looking, and found either nothing or something that wanted to harvest them for using it.

So the money ethic below is not a policy page we wrote afterwards. It is the reason these exist.

The money ethic

Three rules that decide the product, not just the privacy page.

Most software says this in a policy nobody opens. It belongs here, where it can be held against us.

One

Worship is never the paywall

Where the thing being done is learning or worship, access stays free or deliberately cheap. We do not put the fard behind a purchase, and we do not ration it by tier.

Two

Convenience can carry a price

Saved time, sync, advanced tracks, and licences for schools and masajid can be paid for — by the people who can afford them, so that the people who cannot are never asked to.

Three

You are not the inventory

No surveillance advertising, no selling attention, no quiet analytics resale. We have never sold user data and never will. Where an app can run entirely on your own device, it does.

What the labels mean

Three words, defined, so they cannot be stretched.

In development

Real code exists and is being worked on. It is not installable by you yet, and we cannot promise a date we would then have to move.

Private beta

Working software, in the hands of a small number of people who know they are early. Not on any store, not open to the public yet.

Concept

A designed, thought-through idea with nothing built. We list them because we would rather show the whole plan than pretend it is further along.

What is being built, and where each one really is.

The four we stopped

Named, rather than quietly deleted.

Almost nobody publishes what they killed, which is exactly why it is worth publishing. These rows stay so that anyone who saw the old page can see what became of them — and so that the three above are believable.

One reason, and it covers all four

Seven apps and two people was a list, not a plan. None of these was stopped for being a bad idea. They were stopped because attention is the only thing this house is genuinely short of, and splitting it seven ways was going to produce seven things nobody could rely on.

Three of the four had real code written. One never got past a sketch. All four are stopped rather than paused — "paused" is what people say when they mean stopped and would rather not admit it.

NoorReader نور
Study reader A serious PDF and EPUB reader for Islamic texts, with reminders built into the reading itself rather than bolted on as notifications.
No longer being built
Asanīd أسانيد
Chains of transmission A map of the isnād — every scholar and ijāza as one connected graph, every madrasah as a verified page, and your own study recorded as a node that grows it forward. Fully designed, deliberately unbuilt: it needs scholarly partners before it needs code.
No longer being built
Sunrise Masjid
A game · working title A 2.5D platformer about Muslim life — African American, West African, and the wider ummah. The first level is a walk to the masjid at Fajr in South Los Angeles. Our children play games that never once look like them.
No longer being built
Saif al-Ummah سيف الأمة
A game An Islamic action-RPG — regions to cross, a story to carry, and a fight that asks what you are willing to become in order to win it. Playable, and a long way from finished.
No longer being built

If one of these was the one you wanted, say so anyway. Enough people asking is a reason to reconsider, and it is a great deal more useful to us than silence.

Why we ask which one

Seven apps and two people is not a plan, it is a list. Knowing that two hundred of you want one of these and eleven want another is the most useful thing we could possibly learn — it decides what gets built next, and it decides what gets stopped.

So this is not a marketing list. It is the thing that tells us which of these deserve to exist.

What you will not get

Nothing until there is something to open. No launch sequence, no "we are so excited to share", no reminder that we still exist. If an app you ticked gets stopped, you will be told that too — which is the email nobody else sends.
