What a build includes

  • A custom design in light and dark — not a theme with your logo dropped in.
  • Core pages — home, about, programs, events, give, contact — plus what your work needs.
  • Migration off your current platform, with redirects so no existing link breaks.
  • Donations kept on the halal-friendly platform you already trust, not moved.
  • Analytics, search, accessibility and speed treated as part of the job, not extras — plus an automated test suite run before launch, and training for your team.

What a care plan covers

  • Included every month: prayer, jumuʿah and iqāmah times; Ramadan timings; events; announcements; text and photo changes.
  • Hosting, security patches, backups and uptime monitoring. About two business days' turnaround, time-sensitive items first, and no per-item counter.
  • Quoted separately: a whole new page, a campaign page, a new giving flow — priced in writing first. Cancel whenever; nothing switches off, because you already own it.

The arithmetic worth doing before you choose anyone

There are cheaper and even free options, and for a very small masjid one may genuinely be right. But several charge in ways that grow with your community rather than with the work — a cut of every donation, a monthly tier to keep prayer times visible, or a free site you never own.
