A proposal you can read in two minutes

Qur'an classes for your community, adults and children.

Every masjid has adults who pray correctly, raise children in the deen, and were never taught the letters — and children who can still be taught before it becomes the thing they never say out loud. We will run both classes, in separate cohorts, announced by you and taught by us, at no cost to the masjid.

Cost to the masjid
Nothing
No hosting fee, no minimum, no revenue share.
Cost to a student
$50 / month
Cancel any time. Sponsored seats and reduced rates exist.
Format
Live & recorded
Small weekly cohort online. Every session recorded.
What is taught
Read · Tajwīd · Ḥifẓ
Letters to Juz ʿAmma, tajwīd, and memorising the thirtieth juz.

What the masjid does

  • Announces it. From the minbar, the newsletter, the group chat. From you it is an invitation; from us it is an advertisement. Adults and children announced separately.
  • Gives us one point of contact. A person who can answer a question in a day, not a committee.
  • Tells us honestly what is needed. Who is asking, what has been tried, and what did not work.
  • Optionally sponsors seats. Many masajid cover a few students who would not otherwise enrol. Never a condition.

What we do

  • Teach it. Every session, by a former imam — reading from the letters up, tajwīd, and memorising Juz ʿAmma.
  • Place every student. A short call each, out loud — children with a parent — so nobody sits in the wrong class and quietly leaves.
  • Record every session. Students keep the recordings for the weeks they cannot attend.
  • Handle the admin. Sign-ups, reminders, payments and the awkward money conversations — none of it lands on your office.
  • Tell you how it went. Plainly, including if turnout was poor.

Who would be teaching your community

Hamza Abdul-Tawwaab — a former imam who taught Qur'an and led taraweeh before he built anything. He has taught at Islah LA in Los Angeles, Midtown Mosque in Memphis, Masjid As-Sabur in Las Vegas, and Atlanta Masjid in Atlanta. The $50 monthly fee was set on the advice of his own imam and is deliberately below the going rate.

Before anyone is charged

Every student who signs up gets a short call, out loud, and is told honestly where they are starting from — including being told this class is not for them. That is the step most adult programs skip, and it is why most of them lose half their people by week four.

How it goes, start to finish

One
A conversation

Twenty minutes. Who is asking, roughly how many, what has been tried. If it is not a fit we say so on that call.

Two
You announce it

We give you the wording, a link and a date. Nothing to sign.

Three
Everyone is placed

A short call each, out loud. The step most programs skip, and why most adult classes lose half their people.

Four
The cohort starts

Weekly, at an hour that suits the people who enrolled. Join from home, or gather in the building on one screen.

After
You get the truth

How many finished, how many can read who could not, and what we would do differently.

The whole ask

Announce it, give us one contact, and tell us honestly what your people need. There is no fee, no paperwork, and no obligation to continue after the first cohort.
