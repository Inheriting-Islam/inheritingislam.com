The PodcastAn invitation

One conversation, recorded properly

Somebody handed it to you. We would like that recorded.

This is one long conversation with one person about the deen they were given, how they carry it now, and what they intend to leave behind. You do not have to be well known and you do not have to be a scholar. Most of what gets passed on in our communities moves through people who were never on a stage.

How long
One sitting
Usually an hour or two. Breaks whenever you want one.
Where
Your own room
Remote. A link in your browser — nothing to install.
The questions
Sent in advance
All three. There is no fourth one waiting.
Final say
Yours
Nothing is published that you are unhappy with.

The three questions

  • What was handed to you. Who taught you, what house you came up in, what you were given before you were old enough to choose any of it.
  • How you carry it now. The ordinary week, not the highlights. What it costs, and what you have had to work out for yourself.
  • What you are handing forward. To children, students, a community — and what you are afraid will not survive you.

What we will not do

  • We will not edit you into saying something you did not say.
  • We will not ask you to comment on the news, or on anybody else.
  • We will not publish an episode you are unhappy with — including if you change your mind afterwards. That is the end of it, with no argument.
  • You get the recording. We are not asking for exclusivity over your own words.

Why this show exists

Our tradition has always cared who you heard it from. A great deal of what holds our communities together was never written down — it was carried by people who taught, fed, married and buried others for decades, and much of it leaves with them.

The conversation is the point

Every episode gets its own page with the whole thing written out — not a summary. It can be searched, quoted, and found by somebody's grandchild in thirty years.

WrittenNot just heard
Recorded before it is too late

Season one publishes as audio; we record video as well and keep it. You can always take the sound out of a picture. Nobody has ever recovered a face afterwards.

KeptFor your family too

If this is not for you
